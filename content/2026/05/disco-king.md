Title: Cold, Cold Nights Under Chrome and Glass
Date: 2026-05-28
Tags: media

Last week I went to see [The Black Queen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Black_Queen_(band)). This was my first time seeing them perform since 2016, when they were touring [Fever Daydream](https://theblackqueen.bandcamp.com/album/fever-daydream-3). I had no idea that [Danny Lohner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Lohner) had joined the band for this tour until he walked out on stage. At the end of the set I yelled at him to release the [Tapeworm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapeworm_(band)) tapes, you coward.

So if that ever happens, you're welcome.

[Holy Water](https://holywater-music.bandcamp.com/) opened that night. I'd never heard him before, but he was great. He reminds me of [VNV Nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VNV_Nation). I went home and bought [his album](https://holywater-music.bandcamp.com/album/tides), which I did not like as much. He's much better live. He also seems like a swell guy.

This week I went to the [Male Tears](https://maletears.bandcamp.com/) show. I had never heard of them before. They're alright, I guess. Not really my jam. I went because the opener was [Sleek Teeth](https://sleekteeth.bandcamp.com/), who very much is my jam.

I first heard them last Halloween at [Substance Fest](https://gamh.com/shows-substance-2025/). They became my favorite new-to-me band, and my third favorite of the festival overall (after [Sacred Skin](https://sacredskin.bandcamp.com/) and [Pixel Grip](https://pixelgrip.bandcamp.com/)). Their [EP](https://sleekteeth.bandcamp.com/album/sleek-teeth) is only five tracks, but all of them are bangers. When I listen to it I just loop it to make it seem longer -- seven months of that, and I haven't gotten sick of it yet. I like to listen to [Operating](https://sleekteeth.bandcamp.com/track/operating-3) when I'm operating operationally.

They played some new tunes, so I look forward to a full album and more shows. But I will also accept more shows of the same music. Repeatedly.

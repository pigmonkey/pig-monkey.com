Title: Diva
Date: 2026-05-29
tags: media, stationery

Last night I went to a screening of the recent 4K remaster of [Diva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diva_(1981_film)). It looked gorgeous. I hadn't seen Diva for a few years, but was reminded that this may be one of my favorite films. Every scene is dripping with stylish neo-noir coolness. This movie is probably one of the reasons I started going to the opera. Beineix shoots Paris like Mann shoots [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thief_(film)) and [L.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heat_(1995_film)). 

Diva, of course, is also part of the unofficial [Nagra](https://cryptomuseum.com/covert/rec/nagra/index.htm) Film Trilogy. If you like beautifully designed, portable, high-fidelity audio equipment, you want to watch [The Conversation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Conversation), Diva, and [Blow Out](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blow_Out).

And just when you may have thought that this movie couldn't be any more up-my-alley, I noticed during this viewing that in the press conference scene one of the reporters is taking notes on what appears to be a [Rhodia N°13 graph pad](https://rhodiapads.com/collections_orange_13.php) with an (unknown to me) fountain pen. I [appreciate fine stationery](/2025/07/stationery-of-the-jackal/) in film.

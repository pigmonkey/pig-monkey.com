Title: It's Not a Costume
Date: 2026-07-31
Tags: media

I was out of town between mid-June and mid-July, pedaling the hinterland. My last soirée was attending [Elektra](https://www.sfopera.com/operas/elektra/) at the opera, over a month ago. Yesterday, to mark my return to the *beau monde*, I went to the [Kontravoid](https://kontravoid.bandcamp.com/) show. I learned of Kontravoid last year through his [collaboration with Chelsey Crowley](https://kontravoid.bandcamp.com/album/losing-game-remixes) (my favorite Crowley) from [Nuovo Testamento](https://nuovotestamento.bandcamp.com/), of whom I am a fan. Also on the ticket last night was [Normal Bias](https://normal-bias.bandcamp.com/), whose music frequently gets recommended to me, but whose tunes I have never really gotten into.

<a href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/pigmonkey/55434248971/in/dateposted/" title="Kontravoid / Rickshaw Stop"><img src="https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/55434248971_b1e3f54336_c.jpg" width="800" height="533" alt="Kontravoid / Rickshaw Stop"/></a>

Kontravoid was excellent, and I enjoyed Normal Bias more than I thought I would, but my primary reason for going last night was to see the first opener: [Pink Stiletto](https://pinkstiletto.bandcamp.com/). I first met her at one of the shows last Halloween weekend, wherein she complemented my jacket and asked what I was dressing up as. To which I replied: "It's not a costume. I look this good every night."

Title: Kaweco AL Sport
Date: 2026-07-26
Tags: gear, review, stationery, edc

I have previously [maligned the performance of Kaweco pens](/2025/04/pilot-elite/). Despite this I am still a fan of the design of the Kaweco Sport, particularly in matte black aluminum. So when a [Kaweco AL Sport in black](https://www.kaweco-pen.com/en/Kaweco-AL-SPORT-Fountain-Pen-Black/99000025/) appeared on eBay for especially cheap last May, I bought it.

The pen was described as lightly used. The pictures of the eBay listing led me to believe that the body was in great condition, but I was unsure about the nib. Given how cheap the pen was selling for, I assumed that the nib would be damaged. The Sport's nib units in are [readily available](https://www.kaweco-pen.com/en/Kaweco-Nibs-060/99000116/) and easy to replace, so I decided I would preemptively order a new nib before the pen even shipped.

Most critiques of Kaweco pens on The Internets are related to the poor quality control of their nibs. Sometimes you get one that writes great, other times not so much. I avoided this problem by ordering my replacement nib from [The Nibsmith](https://nibsmith.com/), who offers a [tune and smooth](https://nibsmith.com/product/nib-work/) service for free with the purchase of any nib. I ordered a [gold plated Medium nib](https://nibsmith.com/product/kaweco-060-steel-nib-unit-gold-plated/) and a [black Fine nib](https://nibsmith.com/product/kaweco-060-steel-nib-unit-black/). The pen itself was cheap enough that even after purchasing it and these two replacement nibs, I still paid less than the normal list price of the pen.

I also bought a [gold clip](https://www.kaweco-pen.com/en/Kaweco-Clip-Octagonal-Gold-Plated-for-Sport/10000261/), because I believe pens should have clips.

As it turns out, my two preemptive replacement nib purchases were unnecessary. The pen arrived looking like new, with all original packaging. As far as I could tell it was new. One of my two replacement nibs was backordered, delaying the shipment of them both, so I wrote with the Medium nib that came with the pen for about a month. It was perfectly fine. Less scratchy than the Fine nib in my [Kaweco Classic Sport Piston Filler](https://www.kaweco-pen.com/en/Kaweco-PISTON-SPORT-Classic-Black-F/11000335/) -- which may just be attributable to the greater width -- though not quite as pleasant as the gold nibs on either of my Pilot pens.

Writing with the Medium nib reinforced that I do prefer a Fine nib. I like a finer line, and the Medium requires more frequent ink refills. I was glad to have ordered the Fine black nib. I put that one in as soon as I received it and have been writing with it ever since. The Nibsmith tuned it to be silky smooth, and it is now a favorite. The Pilot Vanishing Point remains my favorite pen, but the Kaweco AL Sport now writes equally well, and it has dethroned the Pilot Elite as my favorite compact pocket pen. The need to thread and unthread the cap does still vaguely annoy me, but I love the aesthetics of the Kaweco and the texture of the matte black aluminum.

I did order the [mini converter](https://www.kaweco-pen.com/en/Kaweco-Mini-Converter-foldable/11000369/) for the Kaweco, but as expected it is comically small. I haven't used it once. Instead I refill standard international cartridges with a needle and syringe. I do the same thing with Pilot cartridges in my two Pilot pens.

The one issue I had with the Kaweco is the add-on clip. When installed, it slipped off so easily as to be useless. The same clip works great on the resin body of my Kaweco Classic Sport Piston Filler. I think the aluminum body does not provide enough friction for the clip. To address this, I cut a thin strip of [double-sided tape](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GWQBSV9Q) and placed it around the cap at the position where I wanted the clip. The tape is thin enough that I was able to finagle the band of the clip over it. The clip hasn't moved since, and I can now confidently clip the pen to anything I want.

I expect that my other Nibsmith-tuned nib will eventually find its way into a [Kaweco Brass Sport](https://www.kaweco-pen.com/en/Kaweco-BRASS-SPORT-Fountain-Pen-M/10000918/), if one of those ever shows up on eBay for a low price.
